    About this model

    A walkthrough of the tibial plateau leveling osteotomy, one of the operations most often used for a ruptured cranial cruciate ligament in dogs. It was first described by Barclay Slocum in 1993.

    The stifle is a stylised model, not a scan: a right limb of a dog of about 30 kg with a plateau angle of 26°, cut with a 24 mm blade and turned 21°. It explains the idea and the order of the steps. It is not a surgical guide and not veterinary advice.
